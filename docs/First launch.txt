Droidmount — first launch
=========================

1. Drag Droidmount to the Applications folder.

2. Open it. Droidmount is free and not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate,
   so the first time macOS refuses to open it:
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     - scroll down to "Droidmount was blocked…" and click "Open Anyway",
     - confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   This is needed only once.

3. Droidmount opens its setup window and guides you through the rest:
     - installing macFUSE (included; asks for your password once),
     - allowing the macFUSE file system extension in System Settings,
     - starting Droidmount at login.
   Each step ticks off by itself when it is done.

4. Plug in your phone and choose "File transfer" in the phone's USB notification.
   The phone appears in Finder (and on the desktop) like a USB drive.

Files you copy to the phone are sent when the copy finishes. If sending ever fails,
nothing is lost: the file is kept in "Unsent files" (Droidmount menu) and you get a
notification.

Source code and help: https://github.com/TursiThePanda/droidmount
