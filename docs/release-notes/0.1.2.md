Unplugging and ejecting are instant now, and Eject sticks on Pixel phones.

### Fixed

- **The disk lingered for 10–17 seconds after unplugging the phone** (or after Eject, or when quitting) whenever a folder of the phone was open in Finder. The system `umount` tool first asks the volume for its path, and that request waited behind Finder's and Quick Look's requests until `umount` gave up after about ten seconds. Droidmount now unmounts directly and checks the mount state without asking the phone anything: the disk disappears in well under a second.
- **Eject did not stick on Pixel phones.** A Pixel briefly drops off USB and comes back when Droidmount closes its connection; that looked like plugging it in again, so the phone was mounted again three seconds later. Droidmount now recognises the phone coming back on the same port right after an eject and keeps it ejected until it is really plugged in again.
- **The log file** (`~/Library/Logs/Droidmount.log`, menu → Show log) is appended to again; each launch used to overwrite its beginning.

### Updating

From 0.1.1: Droidmount offers this update by itself (or choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu). From 0.1.0: download the DMG and replace the app once — see the 0.1.1 notes.

Verify the download: `shasum -a 256 Droidmount-0.1.2.dmg` must match `Droidmount-0.1.2.dmg.sha256`.
